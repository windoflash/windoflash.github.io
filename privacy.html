Last updated: August 9, 2026

Windo Flash respects user privacy and is committed to handling information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled by Windo Flash and its applications.

The information collected depends on the features and services provided by each Windo Flash application.

An application may process information such as:

Information may be used to provide application features, maintain application functionality, improve performance, prevent abuse, provide support and comply with legal obligations.

When third-party services are used, their handling of information may be governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

If an application displays advertising, advertising providers may process information as necessary to provide and measure advertisements, subject to their applicable policies and the choices available to users.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Where applicable, information transmitted by an application is protected using appropriate security technologies.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or Google Play requirements.

8. Your Choices

Depending on the application and applicable law, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

9. Account Deletion

If an application provides user account creation, the application will provide an appropriate method for users to request account deletion in accordance with applicable Google Play requirements.
