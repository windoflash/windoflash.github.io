Deen Pulse

Deen Pulse is one of our Islamic applications, created to bring learning and useful everyday Islamic tools together in a simple mobile experience.

The application includes an interactive Islamic quiz covering Islamic knowledge, Quran-related questions, Hadith, Prophets and their stories, Seerah and Islamic history.

It also provides location-based daily prayer times and a digital Tasbeeh counter for tracking Dhikr.

We continue to improve Deen Pulse with additional features, improvements, and updates.
