1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Islamic Quiz 2026 – Deen Pulse, developed and published by Windo Flash.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Deen Pulse application.

We aim to keep the application useful, transparent, and respectful of user privacy.

2. Information We May Access or Collect

The information handled by Deen Pulse depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

2.1 Location Information

Deen Pulse may request access to your device's location to provide location-based prayer times and related functionality.

Location information is used to determine appropriate prayer times for your selected location.

Location access is not required for features that do not need location-based prayer calculations.

Important: If you allow location access, the application may process location information such as geographic coordinates for prayer-time calculations. Depending on the feature implementation, this information may be transmitted to the relevant prayer-time service to obtain calculated prayer times.

2.2 Information Entered by the User

Some features may allow you to enter information such as tracker values, preferences, or other content used by the application.

Such information is intended to support the application's functionality and may be stored locally on your device.

3. App Features

Depending on the version of the application, Deen Pulse may include features such as:

  • Islamic quizzes and educational content
  • Prayer times
  • Location-based prayer-time calculations
  • Tasbeeh / digital counter
  • Duas and Islamic content
  • Deen tracking and personal progress features
  • Notifications and reminders
  • Other Islamic utility features introduced in future versions

4. Prayer Times and Third-Party Services

Deen Pulse may use third-party prayer-time services, including AlAdhan, to calculate or provide prayer times.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the privacy practices of third-party services used by the application where appropriate.
